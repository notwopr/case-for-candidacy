Case for Candidacy: AI Strategist, Legal — David Choi

A concise, structured case for why David Choi is an ideal fit for Perplexity's AI Strategist, Legal role.

David Choi combines high-stakes Delaware legal experience, shipped AI products in regulated domains, and fluency with the tools lawyers use daily. This matches the core need for the AI Strategist, Legal role: someone who understands legal work, can design and evaluate AI systems, and translate between lawyers, product, and engineering.

1. Substantive Legal Expertise ▾

At Abrams & Bayliss, David Choi worked across high-stakes Delaware corporate and commercial matters:

  • Corporate governance & fiduciary duties: merger challenges, fiduciary duty claims, controller buyouts, special committee powers. Example: SynQor fiduciary duty litigation that set precedent on controller buyouts and the business judgment rule.
  • Appraisal & valuation disputes: Zale Merger, Dole Foods, SynQor appraisal matters, including work on valuation documents, search terms, and confidentiality stipulations.
  • LLC & partnership disputes: IDT (LLC amendments, share issuance), Ayres LLC (manager removal, annual meeting procedures), Adir/Curacao dissolution, Vines of Argentina (books and records).
  • Commercial & contract disputes: Fortis Advisors v. iWatt (earnouts, implied covenant), Phillips66 (special equity awards), North Atlantic Holding (engagement letter interpretation), Quantum v. Ploom (confidentiality agreements).
  • Discovery & litigation strategy: complaints, motions, briefs, discovery requests, privilege logs, confidentiality motions, document hold memos, chronologies, and mediation materials.

This shows deep understanding of how complex legal matters unfold and repeated ability to turn messy facts and law into structured analyses, charts, memos, and court filings — exactly the domain knowledge needed to design AI workflows that fit real legal practice.
